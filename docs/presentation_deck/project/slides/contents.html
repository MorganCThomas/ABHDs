Contents: eight questions, each answered in method then result

1

What is in the human family?

110 genes, a lower bound; neither source holds them all

2

Can the backbone locate the nucleophile?

Yes: 31 of 31 known sites; 14 have lost it

3

How alike are catalytic sites?

Very: shared chemistry, not shared substrate

4

Does the ABHD name mark a clade?

A neighbourhood, not a clade

5

Can neighbours predict function?

Only for close neighbours; no gain at 30% identity or less

6

Why are ABHD15 and ABHD5 inactive?

They lost only the nucleophile

7

Do oxyanion holes and pockets separate the panel?

Second donor varies; pocket size partly; no lining signature

8

How robust are the conclusions?

Controls confirm the main results and weaken several effects
