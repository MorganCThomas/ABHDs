Q2 · METHOD

Find the His and acid by structural proximity, then check sequence order

1 Structure

Search in 3D: a His within hydrogen-bond range of the nucleophile; an Asp or Glu within range of that His

2 Sequence

Among candidates, prefer sequence order nucleophile, acid, His (ABHD3: Ser220, Asp346, His375)

3 Name

Name each protein in reaction order (Ser-His-Asp), not sequence order; no nucleophile type at the turn = “no nucleophile”

Source: src/abhd/catalytic.py (triad_and_oxyanion)

The search itself is purely structural (distances in the AlphaFold model). Sequence order is applied afterwards to rank candidate triads; every one of the 93 reported triads has the acid before the His in the sequence, although in space the acid hydrogen-bonds the His and the His contacts the nucleophile. The triad is searched on a predicted model, so side-chain placement is predicted, not observed. Categories describe the site, not activity.
