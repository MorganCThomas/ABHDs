Q7 · METHOD

Measure the active-site cavity on a grid

1 Grid

1 Å grid; points 3.0–3.6 Å from all atoms and buried (≥ 70% of rays blocked) form the cavity

2 Like for like

The elbow residue’s side chain is removed in every protein, so Gly and Ser sites compare fairly

3 Variants

Two models × three grid orientations × three clearances = 18 per anchor

A contrast counts only if its sign holds in all 18 variants.

Source: scripts/21_cavity_robustness.py; src/abhd/structops.py

Models: the CATH-trimmed domain and the full-length AlphaFold model with atoms below pLDDT 50 removed. The grid half-width is 18 Å; some cavities still touch the box edge, so their volumes are lower bounds.
