Follow-up questions

Models versus experiments

How do AlphaFold-based calls compare with PDB structures, and how variable is the active site within and between structures?

Chemistry of the triad

Do QM/MM or machine-learned potentials show differences in nucleophile reactivity and oxyanion stabilisation across the family?

Known substrates

How many proteins have known substrates, what are they, and do they follow structural, pocket or sequence groups?

Predicting the unknown

Which unannotated proteins can be assigned a substrate or activity, and with what confidence?

Ligands and dynamics

Does docking or simulation of lipid substrates show that pocket shape separates the lipid-hydrolase panel?

Roles of the inactive proteins

Do the 14 proteins without a nucleophile share a surface or cap that points to a partner-binding role, and does restoring a Ser restore activity?

Items 1 to 4 come from the project's open questions: validation against experimental structures including conformational variability, quantum or machine-learned potentials for the catalytic chemistry, a census of known substrates mapped onto the groupings found here, and inference for proteins with unknown or unreported substrates, gated by catalytic category. Items 5 and 6 follow from Questions 6 and 7: docking and simulation in the lipid-hydrolase panel, and a comparison of surfaces and caps across the 14 proteins without a nucleophile (including the ABHD4-specific insertion absent from ABHD5), with Ser-restoring mutations as a test.
