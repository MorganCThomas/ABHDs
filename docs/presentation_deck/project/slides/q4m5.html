Q4 · METHOD

Compare the views across families

1 Views

Structure (domain and core), local sequence, FunFam and EC distance

2 Pairs

Spearman correlation of pairwise distances, using only pairs from different paralog groups with no shared FunFam

3 Interval

Bootstrap over paralog groups, 95%

EC distance is one minus the shared EC depth over four levels, for proteins with a complete EC.

Source: scripts/28_multiview.py

Cross-family pairs remove the dominant paralog structure. Sequence and FunFam, and EC with sequence or FunFam, are dependent by construction (FunFam is built from sequence similarity; EC is partly assigned by similarity), so their agreement is not independent confirmation.
