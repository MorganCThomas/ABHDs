Look for a hidden nucleophile, then read the catalytic positions

1 Scan

Test every Ser, Cys, Thr and Asp within three residues of the elbow for a triad

2 Superpose

TM-align ABHD15 on ABHD1/2/3 and ABHD5 on ABHD4

3 Read

Residues at the aligned His and acid positions

Source: scripts/19_inactive_vs_active.py (A1, A2)

The scan is run on every protein; in the 96 proteins that have a nucleophile it serves as a control for how often off-register triads appear.
