Trimmed domain versus full-length: Spearman about 0.7.

Hence only contrasts that hold in every variant are reported.

Source: cavity_variants.csv; like-for-like anchor, clearance 3.2 Å

Within one model the ranking is stable (0.80 to 0.96 across clearance and anchor). Between models it is 0.61 to 0.70. For example ABHD17A and ABHD12 are far larger in the full-length model than in the trimmed domain.
