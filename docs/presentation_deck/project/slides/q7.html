Do oxyanion holes and pockets separate the lipid-hydrolase panel?

Short answer. The second oxyanion donor varies by clade; some pocket-size contrasts are robust; no shared lining signature.

The panel is MGLL, ABHD6, ABHD10, ABHD12, ABHD16A and ABHD2, with ABHD1/3/4/13/14B/17A and LYPLA1 as relatives and controls. All results are on apo AlphaFold models.
