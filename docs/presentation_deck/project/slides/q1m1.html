Q1 · METHOD

Merge two sources, keep one entry per gene

1 Collect

UniProt family query (2025 and 2026) plus human CATH FunFam members

2 Collapse

One reviewed entry per gene; other entries logged with a reason

3 Place

Unnamed fragments aligned to kept genes (≥ 90% identity, ≥ 80% coverage)

Every accession gets a fate, so any drop can be traced.

Source: scripts/07_universe.py; universe_entries.csv

Reviewed (Swiss-Prot) entries with a gene name are canonical. Unreviewed entries with the same gene name are alternative entries; unnamed ones are aligned to the canonical sequences and either assigned to a gene or dropped as unplaced fragments.
