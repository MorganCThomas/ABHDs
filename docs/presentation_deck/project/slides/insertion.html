ABHD4 carries a conserved insertion that ABHD5 lacks

7 of 19 residues have no ABHD5 counterpart.

Pro-Pro-Ala-Trp (187–190) is conserved in ABHD4 orthologues.

Source: orth_segment.csv; 165 ABHD4 and 434 ABHD5 orthologues

This segment sits next to the active site and was the one place where the ABHD5 model differs from ABHD4. It is conserved within ABHD4 orthologues (median 0.76) and 7 of its 19 positions have no aligned residue in ABHD5, so the difference is an ABHD4-specific insertion, not a remodelled ABHD5 surface. A residue-level test suggests pocket-shell divergence, but a run-aware test does not support it (Question 6).
