Within ABHDs, ABHD4 and ABHD5 keep HX₄D, conserved in every orthologue; ABHD1–3 have HX₄E; ABHD15 has neither. The motif follows relatedness, not activity.

Elbow motif: small–X–Nu–X–small holds in 93 of 93 triads (12% at random Ser); strict GXSXG in 75 of 93, and 15 of 18 ABHDs.

Source: motif_audit_*.csv (93 complete triads; ABHD5 His from the TM-align mapping to ABHD4)

Method: for each of the 93 complete triads we read the residue five positions after the catalytic His in the full AlphaFold sequence. HX4D (Asp at His+5) occurs at 12 triad histidines (13%) against 5.3% of other histidines in the same proteins; a one-sided permutation test (one random non-catalytic His per protein) gives p = 0.004, which treats proteins as independent: the 12 hits fall in 10 of 64 FunFams, so the evidence is weaker than the p-value suggests. The enrichment is not specific to +5 in the His loop: Asp frequency is also 17% at His-1, 14% at His-3, 16% at His+2 and 12% at His+8, and about 1% at His+4 and His+6, so the loop is acid-rich with a periodic pattern. Asp or Glu at His+5 occurs at 28 of 93 triad histidines (30%) against 12% at other histidines. The motif Asp is never the triad acid (0 of 93; the acid precedes the His in chain order). The 12 hits: ABHD11, ABHD4, ABHD14B, PPME1, PLA2G7, PAFAH2, EPHX2, EPHX4, OLAH and the serine carboxypeptidases CTSA, CPVL and SCPEP1. Of the six proteins with an EC 2.3 (acyltransferase) record, two (ABHD14B, PAFAH2) have HX4D; PAFAH2 also has a hydrolase EC; Fisher p = 0.17 against the rest, which this sample cannot resolve. Orthologues (cleaned vertebrate sets): the +5 residue is Asp in ABHD4 (D325) and ABHD5 (D332) and Glu in ABHD1, ABHD2 and ABHD3, each conserved in at least 99.8% of orthologues; in ABHD15 it is Arg (84%). ABHD5 has no nucleophile call, so its His (H327) is the residue TM-align places on the ABHD4 His. Elbow motif: the strict GXSXG fails in ABHD14A and ABHD14B (SPSLS), ABHD16A (AWSIG), and in other families; small (G, A, S, C or T) residues two before and two after the nucleophile occur at all 93 triads, against 12% for random Ser residues. The elbow was called by backbone geometry, not sequence, so this is an independent result.
